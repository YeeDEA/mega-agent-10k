## 🌌 The Narrative & Motivation

In an era dominated by trillion-parameter monolithic reasoning engines (**Fable 5.1**, **Claude Opus 5.5**, **GPT-6 Astra**), this research artifact addresses a foundational question in distributed AI systems:

> *"Can an ensemble of open-weight **GPT-OSS 120B** agents, orchestrated across 100 discrete evolutionary stages under strict deterministic verification, synthesize rigorous academic research, functional production software, and closed-loop agent-based simulations that exceed single-turn monolithic reasoning?"*

This repository proves that **orchestration topology, rigorous red-test verifiers, and multi-agent consensus** unlock extraordinary emergence. Across 10,000 parallel subagent rounds, the system designed and verified an entire smart city architecture spanning transportation grids, renewable energy, and vertical agrarian clusters.
